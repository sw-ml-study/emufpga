Built components/format/: the .spm container, in six no_std-where-possible crates (spm-bytes, spm-header, spm-codec, spm-layout, spm-walk, spm-file). Ternary codes chosen so bit 0 is the accumulator enable and bit 1 the subtract select, making the stream drive the arithmetic unit with no decode stage; code 10 left permanently invalid so the hardware decoder stays combinational and doubles as a corruption check. Scales precede the weights they cover so the engine never seeks for one; groups are byte-aligned; no offset table anywhere. SpmReader exposes no seek even privately, and hands back packed bytes rather than decoded weights to match TensorSink::consume. Extracting spm-walk (a forward-only (stream, group) cursor shared by reader, writer and the step 003 WeightStream) fixed a real bug where the reader reported a group's stream index after advancing past it. 35 tests including a golden fixture built by hand in Python from docs/spm-format.md rather than emitted by the writer, so the byte-for-byte check compares two independent readings of the spec. Also corrected step 001's docs: sw-checklist warns above 4 functions/module and 4 modules/crate, so the documented target of 5 guaranteed a warning.
